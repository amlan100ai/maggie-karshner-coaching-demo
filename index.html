About Maggie

Coaching built on real experience

Maggie Karshner has spent over 20 years coaching solo entrepreneurs and creative professionals through the real work of building a self-sustaining business — not just chasing a passion, but turning it into something that actually pays. Her work has been featured on KING 5's Evening Magazine and in The Seattle Times. Maggie also works with Washington State unemployment recipients through the state's Self-Employment Assistance Program (SEAP).
